📝 GRAMMAR CONTEXT (Page 91):

"Now that we know how to say we HAVE DONE something, what if someone asks exactly HOW LONG we have done it? We need two magic words: Since and For."
Action: Explain the visuals clearly. 'Since' is a starting point (a calendar date/clock). 'For' is a measuring tape (amount of time).

📏

Part 2: Since vs. For

📍

📍 SINCE

A Starting Point in Time

  • ...since 2018
  • ...since yesterday
  • ...since I was eight
  • ...since lunchtime
⏳

⏳ FOR

A Duration (Amount of time)

  • ...for ten years
  • ...for two days
  • ...for a long time
  • ...for three months

Part 6: The Colombia Tourist Vlog

Imagine you are a tourist visiting a city in Colombia. Write a vlog script for your YouTube channel!

Vlog Structure:

  • 1. Greeting: "Hey guys! We are in [City]!"
  • 2. How long (Present Perfect): "We have been here for [Time] / since [Day]."
  • 3. What you did (Simple Past): "Yesterday, we visited... We ate..."
  • 4. Opinion: "It was amazing / exhausting / beautiful!"
